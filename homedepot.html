DIY Project & Material Planner

Plan it. Price it. Build it, with the right materials in the right order.

Product Brief: DIY Project & Material Planner

Prepared by product management for merchandising, e-commerce, and store operations leadership. Sample data.

Objective

Turn the Home Depot search box into a project advisor. The planner converts a project idea and a few measurements into a complete, code-aware material basket priced for the customer's province, then hands that basket to the online cart or a printable store list. Search captures one item; the planner captures the whole job.

Business case

  1. Basket builders. A complete-materials checklist is the purest basket builder in the funnel. The customer arrives for one sheet of plywood and leaves with the screws, brackets, edge banding, and finish the job actually requires. Every line the planner adds is a unit the customer would otherwise discover at the counter, or at a competitor on the second trip.
  2. Cross selling and cross bundling. Lists are sequenced in build order, which creates natural attach points: structure sells fasteners, fasteners sell finish, finish sells application tools, and every stage sells the rental that does the heavy lifting. Bundles mirror how pros buy, so the basket reads as expert rather than padded.
  3. Upselling. Good, Better, and Best grade cards put the whole trade-up decision on one screen. The Most Popular flag steers customers to the middle grade, lifting average order value without a single discount.
  4. Low margin anchors. Lumber and commodity boards are thin-margin traffic drivers. The planner keeps the anchor price honest and visible, then recovers margin on the attachments: fasteners, finishes, safety gear, tool rental, and delivery.
  5. Perception drivers on key SKUs. Lists feature house brands customers can only buy here (Behr, Rust-Oleum Varathane, HDX, Ryobi), so every plan builds equity in SKUs the company controls, and the finish line doubles as a merchandising slot for priority SKUs.
  6. Seasonal products. The project lineup rotates with the season: decks and fences lead spring and summer, interior paint and storage lead fall and winter. The planner turns seasonal campaigns into high-intent, full-basket sessions.

Business requirements

  • Province-aware pricing, sales tax, and delivery thresholds
  • Cart handoff online plus a printable list for store pickup
  • Pro Xtra capture at print and handoff
  • French (FR-CA) parity for Québec
  • Legal framing: planning estimates, never professional or permit advice
  • Merchandising control of grade definitions and featured SKUs
  • Seasonal rotation of the project lineup from the CMS
  • Store locator integration for pickup eligibility
  • Customer support playbook for plan-related questions

Technical requirements

  • Pricing service integration with the provincial tax engine and store price lists
  • Store-level inventory and availability (ATP) checks
  • Headless CMS for project content, lists, and seasonal rotation
  • Analytics: basket composition, grade mix, attach rates, funnel events
  • A/B testing for card order, flags, and copy
  • WCAG 2.1 AA, saved state across sessions, and a print stylesheet
